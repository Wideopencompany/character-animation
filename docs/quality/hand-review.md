# Hand acceptance rules

User-visible anatomy and movement are separate gates. New finger bones alone
do not establish that a hand has improved visually or moves in the viewer.
Review both hands open and flexed, with realistic body distance and accessible
close-up framing. Verify cuffs in Walk/Run. A hand repair must preserve original
face/body texture and existing clip data. Label inspection clips truthfully;
do not rename a static rest pose as Idle or claim Conversation from finger flex.
Always show new asset hashes and an actual public interaction before reporting
the repair complete. Changing character assets must surface unavailable actions
explicitly; previously functioning actions cannot be silently called preserved.
